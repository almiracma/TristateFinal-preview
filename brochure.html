Use the Prima touchscreen or phone app to check your security system, view cameras and operate connected home devices. The panel can be wall mounted or placed on a tabletop.

What you can do from the touchscreen

Check security status

See whether doors and windows are open or closed, and use the panel’s Staying and Leaving controls.

Operate home controls

Control compatible lights and other connected devices, with settings organized by room.

Adjust climate settings

View room temperature and adjust a compatible thermostat from the touchscreen.

Check the weather

The same touchscreen also displays weather information, alongside your security and home controls.
